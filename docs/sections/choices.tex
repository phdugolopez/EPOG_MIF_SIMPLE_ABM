\section{Choices left open by the briefs}\label{sec:choices}

The briefs give a starting rule for each mechanism; version 1 needed further choices. The
full list, with status (fixed at planning or default), alternatives and owner, is
\code{docs/design/choices.md}; \code{docs/design/} is the design reference, and changes are
proposed there. The main ones:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Initial vintages.} One plant of age 0 per initial firm: all retire together at
  $t = 9$, which leaves echo cycles every ten periods.
\item \textbf{Okishio hurdle floored at zero} in investment, replacement and entry. Without
  the floor, $h < 0$ in a deep price war would make replacement easier for Okishio firms
  than for Shaikh firms.
\item \textbf{Non-renewal at firm level}: a firm leaves when its last plant retires with a
  lifetime rate below $\rbar$; read at plant level, it would change the investment rule.
\item \textbf{Debt cap and losses.} In version 1 the cap (twice sales revenue) binds
  borrowing for plants; version 2 (\code{BORROWING = False}) pays for plants from cash only.
  Losses are covered by an overdraft outside the cap. Firms repay 10\,\% of debt a period.
\item \textbf{Overtime premium} of 10\,\% on output above normal capacity, added to the
  brief's profit equation.
\item \textbf{A plant runs only if the price covers its operating cost}: sales are capped at
  the output offered at the posted price.
\item \textbf{Ties are shared pro rata} in the merit order and the overflow: the paired
  firms start identical, so ordering by firm would favour one type.
\item \textbf{The entry trigger} is the realised rate on recent plants (the brief says both
  ``realised'' and ``at the current price'').
\item \textbf{Open values:} $u^{war} = 0.95$, $\lambda = 0.25$, $c = 0.02$, $b = 0.025$,
  $\rho = 0.05$, $f_{\min} = 0.01$, entrant undercut 1\,\%, logit $\eta = 1$, initial price
  100, initial cash and debt zero.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Adding an alternative rule.} Write the new equation in its own file (or as a
second function in the mechanism's file), add a switch to \code{parameters.py} with the
version 1 rule as default, branch on it in the scheduler step, add a test, and record the
choice in \code{docs/design/choices.md}.
